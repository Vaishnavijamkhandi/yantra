D3COY 1.0 – Problem Statement 03
AI-BASED CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE CYBER-RISK PREDICTION

PROBLEM STATEMENT
Critical infrastructure includes power grids, water treatment, transportation, telecommunications, hospitals, and smart-city systems. These systems depend on interconnected digital and operational technologies, making them exposed to cyber threats through IoT devices, cloud infrastructure, ICS/OT systems, and vendor dependencies.

A single vulnerability or compromised supplier can affect connected systems, causing service disruption, financial loss, and public-safety risks.

The system must understand not just individual vulnerabilities, but also asset criticality, interdependencies, exposure, and the possible impact of compromise.

CHALLENGE
Develop an AI-powered cyber-risk prediction and prioritisation system that evaluates the security posture of critical infrastructure using simulated infrastructure data, vulnerability information, network activity, and asset criticality.

The system should identify vulnerable components, estimate risk, predict assets needing urgent attention, and recommend preventive measures before incidents escalate.

KEY OBJECTIVES
1. Infrastructure Asset Profiling
   Represent critical assets including power-grid systems, water treatment plants, hospitals, IoT devices, and industrial control systems with importance and dependencies.

2. Vulnerability Assessment
   Analyse asset inventories, software versions, configuration weaknesses, and exposed services to find security gaps.

3. AI-Based Risk Prediction
   Use machine learning or risk modelling to estimate likelihood and impact of cyber incidents using historical or simulated data.

4. Risk Prioritization
   Rank assets according to severity, exposure, operational importance, and consequences of compromise.

5. Dependency and Impact Analysis
   Identify how compromised components can affect connected assets and essential services.

6. Predictive Risk Dashboard
   Visualise risk scores, vulnerable assets, threat levels, and urgent attention items.

7. Preventive Recommendations
   Suggest actions such as patch prioritization, segmentation, access control, monitoring, and incident response planning.

EXPECTED SOLUTION
- Shift from reactive vulnerability management to proactive risk assessment
- Provide a working prototype with asset inventory, vulnerability info, AI-generated risk scores, and recommendations
- Explain why assets are high-risk and how fixing a vulnerability reduces overall infrastructure risk

PROJECT GOAL
Build a working prototype that demonstrates:
- asset inventory and criticality
- vulnerability matching and prioritisation
- risk score generation
- explainable recommendations
- dashboard presentation
- experimental ML-based threat prioritisation

SUCCESS CRITERIA
A solution should be considered complete when it can:
- model assets and their criticality
- correlate assets with vulnerabilities
- generate risk scores
- prioritize assets by urgency
- show risk explanations
- provide actionable recommendations
- present all of this in a clear dashboard or interface

IMPORTANT NOTES
- This is a prototype and decision-support platform, not a production scanner.
- Vulnerability matching should be conservative and explainable.
- ML outputs should be treated as experimental support, not final operational truth.
- The system should be evaluated with realistic, simulated data and clear assumptions.
